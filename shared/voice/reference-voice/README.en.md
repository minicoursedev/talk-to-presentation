# TTS reference-tone catalog

The handoff includes only this catalog and its transcripts, not reference WAVs. Recipients must record or supply audio they have the right to use, and update the filenames and actual transcripts in the canonical `README.md` together. Only the tones used by a case are needed; the complete catalog is not required.

The tone IDs, WAV filenames, and recording transcripts below are intentionally preserved in Traditional Chinese: they are runtime data, not translated instructions. The English glosses are explanatory only. Do not record the English gloss while keeping a Chinese reference transcript. The tool reads the canonical `README.md`, not this translation.
